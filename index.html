Questions people ask

Myths and straight answers

Is network marketing a scam?

A legitimate company pays you for real products sold to real customers. If money only comes from recruiting people and nobody uses the products, walk away. NeoLife has been selling nutrition products since 1958.

Do I need to be good at talking to people?

No. Most of our team started shy. The training gives you simple words to use, and you can share online through WhatsApp and social media.

Can I do this alongside school or a job?

Yes. Most people start part-time, with a few hours a week, and grow from there.

I'm not in Nigeria. Can you still sponsor me?

If NeoLife operates in your country, yes. Pick your country at the top of the page. If it isn't listed, message me and I'll check for you.

How much does it cost to start?

It depends on your country's starter options. Message me and I'll send you the current options for where you live.
